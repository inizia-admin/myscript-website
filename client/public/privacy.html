My Script

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026
Developer: Micaela Piacenza · My Script

Your Content Stays With You

Scripts, preferences, review-prompt counters and unfinished recording recovery files are stored locally on your device. Finished videos are saved to Photos on iOS or your gallery on Android. My Script does not upload your scripts, camera images, audio or videos to developer servers, and has no account system, advertising or developer analytics SDK.

Permissions

The camera is used for the preview and recording. The microphone captures recording audio and an on-device level indicator. Photo-library or storage permission is used to save videos. Supported motion sensors provide local framing and orientation information. You can manage permissions in your device settings.

Storage And Deletion

Scripts and preferences remain until you delete them, clear app data or uninstall the app. Completed recovery files are removed after a successful save; unfinished takes remain locally for recovery. Saved gallery videos remain until you delete them in Photos or your gallery. Your operating system may back up or sync files according to your own backup and photo settings; My Script has no developer-operated cloud sync.

Store Services And Support

Apple, Google Play and your operating system handle purchases, native review prompts and any store diagnostics under their own privacy policies. Reviews you choose to submit go to the store and may be public, or shared privately with the developer during closed testing; you can delete reviews through the store. My Script does not receive your payment-card details. If you email hello@inizia.agency, we receive what you choose to send and use it to respond to your enquiry; contact us there with privacy or deletion requests.

Delete your data

To delete scripts and preferences, delete them in My Script or clear the app's data or uninstall it. Delete saved videos separately in Photos or your Android gallery. Local files are not held by the developer.

To remove a Google Play review, open My Script's page in Google Play, find your review, tap More, then Delete. Google's review-deletion instructions explain how; Google manages store data under its own policies.

For deletion requests concerning support messages or feedback you sent to us, email hello@inizia.agency with the subject “My Script data deletion” and identify the message or feedback to remove. These requests concern our copies of your correspondence and feedback, not your on-device scripts or gallery. Records that must be kept to meet legal obligations may be retained for the period those obligations require.

Questions about privacy? Contact us.
